About ALE

The Aggregate Lifespan Encyclopedia integrates multiple longevity databases including the Amniote Life History Database , AnAge , Malddaba , MIDAS , the Max-Planck longevity records , PanTHERIA , the Primate Ageing Database , Species360 , and data from independent colonies. When curating ALE our goal was to provide curated maximum lifespan independent of sample size. In our analysis we found a correlation between zoo sample size and maximum lifespan. Downstream genomics work would therefore be confounded by this correlation. To that end this database includes new metrics(Q90, E90 and a boot-strap maximum lifespan) with reduced correlations to sample size. When possible, boot-strap maximums were calculated for a given species and used as the curated maximum value. In cases of animals known to struggle in captive conditions, like bats and whales, we instead used a combination of established data and wild data to fill in our curated maximum lifespan.

Example representation of ALE curation. Here we graph maximum lifespans from major sources that contributed to creation of database: Max-Planck longevity records, Amniote life history database, and AnAge database. Further included are box plots to highlight distributions of various sources of data. In this example we show the distribution of all Bos Taurus as well as sex segregated distributions. In this example the highest maximum lifespan of 36 years is not representative of most cows, and was only achieved after over 2000 estimations. Instead, the boot-strap maximum lifespan of 23 years is used for the curated dataset. Despite being lower than the highest maximum lifespan, the boot-strap maximum is still greater than the previous maximum of 20 years recorded in the Amniote life history database, AnAge, and the Max-Planck longevity records.
